## Download

| Your computer | Download |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10 or 11** | `KeywordTracker-Windows.exe` |
| **Mac with Apple Silicon** (M1, M2, M3, M4…, most Macs from 2021 on) | `KeywordTracker-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip` |
| **Mac with an Intel chip** (older Macs) | `KeywordTracker-Mac-Intel.zip` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Click the Apple menu  → **About This Mac**. It says "Chip: Apple M…" or "Processor: Intel".

The files are under **Assets** at the bottom of this page.

## Windows

1. Download `KeywordTracker-Windows.exe` and double-click it.
2. The first time, Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"**. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. (This appears because the app isn't from a big company that pays for a signing certificate. It only happens once.)
3. A small black window opens and Keyword Tracker appears in your web browser after a few seconds.
4. **Keep the black window open** while you use it. Closing it stops Keyword Tracker.

Tip: move the .exe somewhere permanent (like your Desktop or Documents) before running it, and run it from there each time.

## Mac

1. Download the right `.zip` for your Mac and double-click it to unzip.
2. Drag **Keyword Tracker** into your **Applications** folder, then open it.
3. The first time, macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software. Click **Done**. Then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to Keyword Tracker. Enter your password if asked. You only do this once.
4. Keyword Tracker opens in your web browser after a few seconds. There's no window or Dock icon; the browser page is the app.
5. To stop it, click **Quit** at the top right of the page.

## Good to know

- Everything stays on your computer. Nothing is shared with anyone.
- Weekly updates only run while the app is open. If it was closed at update time, it updates as soon as you open it again.
- To open the page again later, just open the app again. If it's already running, it brings the page back up.
- Your data is kept in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\KeywordTracker` on Windows and `~/Library/Application Support/KeywordTracker` on Mac. Deleting that folder resets everything.
